PLAY ON YOUR PHONE

FULLSCREEN · OFFLINE · HOME SCREEN APP
▸ OPEN THE GAMEthen follow the steps below

IPHONE / IPAD

  1. Open the game in Safari (the button above).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch Holy Poker from your home screen — it opens fullscreen, no browser bars.
  5. Rotate to landscape — that's how the game is meant to be played.

ANDROID

  1. Open the game in Chrome (the button above).
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app if offered), then confirm.
  4. Launch it from your home screen and rotate to landscape.
☩ It works offline. After the first launch, the whole game is stored on your phone — you can play anywhere, no connection needed. Your progress saves on your device and is never lost, even when you lose a run.

TIPS AT THE TABLE

  1. Tap up to 5 cards to select them for a hand.
  2. Drag sideways to reorder cards — scoring runs left to right.
  3. Hold your finger on a card to inspect its ability.
  4. Beat the chip target before your hands run out. Then visit the shop, take a blessing, and go deeper.
